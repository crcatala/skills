Style examples

Each row is one template from styles.md, filled with the same subject for each of three hypothetical apps. Results vary between runs; these show the general look.

  • Harvest Ledger: a green seedling sprouting from an open ledger book, with a small ripe tomato resting on the pages
  • PulseCare: a rounded red heart with a white medical cross in its center and a stethoscope looped around it
  • Tidewater Freight: a small cargo ship carrying a stack of colorful shipping containers, riding a single curling ocean wave
Harvest LedgerAgriculture: Harvest and produce tracking for small farmsPulseCareHealthcare: Clinic appointments and patient vitalsTidewater FreightLogistics: Container shipping and route tracking
