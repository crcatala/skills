This repository has reduced the guide to a browser QA policy: tool selection,
a task-specific session on every shell invocation, and verification/evidence
standards. Command catalogs and detailed recipes are no longer bundled; the
policy directs agents to installed CLI references instead. Attribution from
the earlier guide is retained rather than asserting that reducing or rewording
it removes provenance.

Official guides fetched through `agent-browser skills get` are supplied by the
installed CLI and remain subject to their own upstream terms.
